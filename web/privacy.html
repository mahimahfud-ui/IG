Mahi Social Cleaner Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 7, 2026

What the extension does

Mahi Social Cleaner is a browser-side utility for accounts you control. It can read supported Instagram web data from the tab where you are already signed in and help you review selected activity before taking an action.

Passwords and session data

The extension does not ask users to enter an Instagram password. It does not send Instagram cookies, session tokens, or account credentials to the Mahi server.

Local data

The extension stores only local interface preferences, such as compact mode and reduced motion. Lists returned from an active scan remain in the extension popup session and are not uploaded by the extension.

Third-party services

The extension opens Instagram and official/public project pages when requested. Instagram is operated by Meta and is not affiliated with this project. InstaPy and other referenced community projects are independent third-party projects.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the extension's behavior changes. The current policy will remain available at this URL.
